# Metastatic Pancreatic Cancer Trials

Date checked: April 12, 2026
Source: ClinicalTrials.gov live study records

Criteria:
- metastatic pancreatic cancer
- currently recruiting
- second-line or after progression on standard chemotherapy
- U.S. sites only
- West Coast priority

## West Coast Priority

### NCT06790602
Title: Cemiplimab Plus Gemcitabine in Patients With Metastatic Pancreatic Adenocarcinoma
Link: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06790602
Status: Recruiting
U.S. sites: California
Why it fits:
- metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma
- for patients already treated with FOLFIRINOX or gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel
- biomarker-restricted: SWI/SNF alterations required

### NCT07049055
Title: A Clinical Trial to Evaluate EDV Nanocell Therapy With Gemcitabine and Nab-paclitaxel in Pancreatic Cancer
Link: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07049055
Status: Recruiting
U.S. sites: California, New Jersey, New York, Ohio
Why it fits:
- metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma
- documented progression on first-line FOLFIRINOX or NALIRIFOX

### NCT06998940
Title: Studying Chemotherapy With or Without Panitumumab for Unresectable, Locally Advanced, or Metastatic Pancreatic Cancer Without KRAS Mutations
Link: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06998940
Status: Recruiting
U.S. sites: California, Oregon, Washington, plus many others
Why it fits:
- includes metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma
- one prior line of systemic chemotherapy
- disease progressed, was refractory, or intolerant
Important caveat:
- not metastatic-only
- KRAS wild-type required
